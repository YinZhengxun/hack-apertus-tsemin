You compare two language versions of the same Swiss government web page. The REFERENCE below is the complete {{REF_LANG}} version. The BLOCKS are numbered pieces of the {{BLOCK_LANG}} version. The two versions are supposed to say the same thing, but they often differ in small ways.

For each block, decide:
- "covered": everything in the block is expressed somewhere in the reference with the same meaning. Different word order, different sentence splitting, ordinary translation choices and grammar do not count as differences.
- "absent": the block as a whole has no counterpart in the reference (for example a sentence, heading, remark, list item or link text that exists only in this version).
- "partly": most of the block is expressed in the reference, but some words carry meaning that the reference does not express, or expresses differently. List those words in "quotes".

Rules for "quotes":
- Copy the words character by character from the block, in their original language. Never translate, correct or complete them.
- Quote only the words that carry the difference, as few as possible: typically 1 to 6 words. A number, a name, an abbreviation, an adjective, a verb or a short phrase. Do not quote whole sentences.
- Annotators of this task mark even small shifts in meaning: a more specific or more general term, a different verb, an added qualifier, information that is only in one version. Mark them too.
- One block can have several quotes.
